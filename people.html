Principal investigator

Ioannis Mandralis

Assistant Professor in Robotics

Dr. Mandralis received his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from the California Institute of Technology, where he worked with Morteza Gharib and Richard M. Murray as a member of the Center for Autonomous Systems and Technologies (CAST). He is currently a Postdoctoral Scholar at EPFL with Mirko Kovač, where his research focuses on the design, dynamics, and control of aerial and multi-modal robotic systems, with an emphasis on developing novel capabilities for terrestrial, aerial, and aquatic locomotion. He received his B.Sc. from EPFL and his M.Sc. from ETH Zurich in Mechanical Engineering, where his earlier research centered on learning and computational methods in fluid dynamics and robotics. He is a recipient of the Onassis Foundation Scholarship for his doctoral research, the Rolf D. Buhler Memorial Award for exemplary academic performance and the Hans G. Hornung Prize for his Ph.D. defense at Caltech, and the Degree Distinction Prize from ETH Zurich.
