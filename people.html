Principal investigator

Ioannis Mandralis

University Assistant Professor in Robotics

I am a University Assistant Professor in Robotics in the Machine Intelligence Group of the Division of Information Engineering at the University of Cambridge. I obtained my PhD in the Center for Autonomous Systems and Technology from Caltech and was subsequently a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL. I am interested in giving robots the agility and intelligence they need to do useful physical work in the real world.
