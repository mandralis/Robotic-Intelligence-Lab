Openings

I am starting a new group at the University of Cambridge in January 2027, working on embodied AI for multimodal robotics. I am looking for people who want to work at the point where robot learning, control theory, and real robot hardware meet.

Where the group is going, and the work it builds on, is described on the Research page. Read those first — they will tell you whether this is the right place for you.

PhD students

I expect to take on PhD students starting in October 2027, admitted through the Department of Engineering. Strong backgrounds in any of control theory, reinforcement learning, aerial robotics, dynamics, or robot systems engineering are a good fit.

Funding routes include departmental studentships, Cambridge Trust scholarships, and external fellowships. I am glad to support strong candidates through these applications.

Master’s and project students

I welcome enquiries from Master’s thesis students, Part IIB and MPhil project students, and visiting researchers. Projects are available on morphing flight, multi-modal planning, learning-based control, and soft aerial systems. Briefs for open projects are listed under each direction on the Research page.

Postdocs

I do not have funded postdoctoral positions open yet, but I am happy to discuss fellowship applications — Marie Skłodowska-Curie, Royal Society, EPSRC, and college research fellowships among them — with researchers whose interests overlap with the group.

Getting in touch

Email me with your CV, your transcript, and a short paragraph on your research interests. Please put “PhD 2027” or “Master’s project” in the subject line: imm38@cam.ac.uk.
